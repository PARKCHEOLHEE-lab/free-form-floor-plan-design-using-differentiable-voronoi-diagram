Free-form Floor Plan Design

via a differentiable Voronoi diagram — the Rust optimizer compiled to WebAssembly, running live in your browser

Draw a boundary (or pick a preset), choose how many rooms and how the loss is weighted, then watch Voronoi sites move under central finite-difference gradients and AdamW until the cells settle into rooms. Every step — the Voronoi diagram, the six loss components, the gradients — runs in-browser. No server.
